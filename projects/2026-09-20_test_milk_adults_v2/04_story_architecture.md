# Story Architecture

Dependency order is binding:
1. sữa/đường are known.
2. define lactose = loại đường tự nhiên có trong sữa.
3. introduce lactase afterward = chất ruột non tạo ra để xử lý lactose.
4. do not introduce enzyme unless later reasoning truly needs the label.
5. do not use lactase-persistence or selection-pressure labels.

Narrative beats:
- baby/adult contradiction;
- pottery evidence;
- culture-before-biology reveal;
- explain multi-generation advantage in plain action terms;
- complicate with 2022 famine/disease model;
- payoff with milk glass as culture ↔ biology interaction.
