# Story Architecture

## Central question
Vì sao những cục vôi trắng có thể giúp một số Roman concrete lấp vết nứt, và evidence nào cho thấy người La Mã thực sự dùng hot mixing?

## Beats
1. **Object hook:** đốm trắng từng bị coi là lỗi.
2. **Reverse assumption:** 2023 chemistry suggests function.
3. **Mechanism:** vôi sống + nước → reactive calcium reservoir.
4. **Experiment:** deliberately cracked replicas; water stops after ~2 weeks.
5. **Complication:** mechanism is convincing, but did Romans really mix this way?
6. **Archaeological payoff:** unfinished Pompeii site 79 CE preserves dry premix; 2025 study.
7. **Guardrail ending:** not magic/not all Roman concrete; callback to white speck.

## Concept placement
Explain vôi sống by origin/action. Explain “trộn nóng” only after action. Do not use pozzolan/lime clast jargon in final.

## Scope
IN: hot mixing, crack filling, 2023 + 2025 evidence.
OUT: all Roman engineering, commercial modern concrete, climate claims.

## Duration
~3.5 minutes.
