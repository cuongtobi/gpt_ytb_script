# 04 — Story + Knowledge Architecture

Central question: why does cutting, rather than merely possessing, an onion trigger tears?

Story beats:
1. contradiction: intact onion vs cut onion;
2. separated cellular ingredients;
3. alliinase and short-lived sulfur intermediates;
4. 2002 LFS discovery;
5. volatile tear factor reaches the eye;
6. reflex tears as protection;
7. plant-defense logic and variation;
8. experimental low-tear onions;
9. practical exposure logic;
10. concise causal payoff.

Knowledge order: common subject → enzyme concept → alliinase → LFS → named volatile compound → reflex tears.

Reveal rule: the 2002 enzyme discovery is teased only after the cellular mechanism has been established.
