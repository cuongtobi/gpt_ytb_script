# 02 — Focused Research Notes

This multilingual fixture intentionally reuses one source/evidence set.

Key findings:
- intact onion tissue separates sulfur precursors from alliinase sufficiently to prevent the rapid damage-triggered pathway;
- cutting disrupts cells and alliinase generates reactive sulfenic-acid intermediates;
- Imai et al. (2002) identified lachrymatory-factor synthase as a specific enzyme producing the tear factor;
- later work identifies syn-propanethial S-oxide as the volatile lachrymatory factor and studies LFS catalysis;
- irritation triggers protective reflex tears;
- suppressing LFS can strongly reduce tear-factor formation in experimental onion lines;
- onion organosulfur chemistry is connected with pungency and chemical defense.

Uncertainty discipline:
- do not claim every onion has identical precursor levels;
- do not claim a kitchen trick completely stops the chemistry;
- do not universalize results from experimental tearless onion lines.
