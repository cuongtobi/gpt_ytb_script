# 01 — Angle Engine

Selected angle: contradiction + mechanism.

Central question: Why can an intact onion sit harmlessly in a kitchen, while cutting it creates an airborne irritant within seconds?

Transformation: intact separated cell chemistry → tissue damage → enzyme reactions → volatile tear factor → eye irritation → reflex tears.

Scope: onion tear chemistry and the human protective response. Modern health claims, nutrition claims and exhaustive cooking advice are out of scope.

This fixture uses the same factual spine across six languages so language behavior, not topic choice, is the test variable.
