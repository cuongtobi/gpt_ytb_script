# Research
- OSHA: electric shock occurs when the body becomes part of a circuit; current enters at one point and leaves at another.
- DOE/APLIC material: avian electrocution occurs when birds bridge two energized parts or an energized part and grounded hardware.
- Higher-voltage transmission lines can present lower electrocution risk to large birds when conductor/ground spacing is greater; this does not mean high voltage is safer.
- BBC Science Focus gives the standard potential-difference explanation for birds on one conductor.
