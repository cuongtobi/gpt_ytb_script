# Research
- NOAA/USGS: evaporation removes water from ocean surface while salts remain behind.
- USGS water cycle: ocean evaporation supplies much atmospheric water; precipitation returns water to land/ocean.
- USGS/NOAA: ocean salts largely come from weathering of rocks and dissolved material carried by rivers; seafloor sources also contribute.
- Ocean salinity is affected by both inputs and removal processes.
- Rain is not absolutely pure; marine aerosols and atmospheric material can add dissolved substances.
