# 04 — Story + Knowledge Architecture

## Central question
Bằng cách nào một kẻ săn mồi họ hàng với sói đã trở thành loài vật đầu tiên sống trong quan hệ thuần hoá với con người?

## Core transformation
sói hoang cạnh tranh → cá thể chịu được khoảng cách với người → quần thể sống gần người → chó cổ đã phân hoá → chó lan giữa cộng đồng → tiếp tục thích nghi khi xã hội người thay đổi.

## Beats
- **B01 Hook (1:30):** phá hình ảnh “một người bắt một con sói”. TEASE C001/C002/C007.
- **B02 Shared world (2:00):** người và sói cùng là thợ săn; giải thích domestication đa thế hệ. EXPLAIN C001/C011.
- **B03 Archaeological ambiguity (2:20):** xương khó phân loại; Bonn-Oberkassel. EVIDENCE C008.
- **B04 Genome opens the tree (2:30):** genome, five lineages by 11 ka, 2022 ancient wolves. EVIDENCE/COMPLICATE C005/C006/C007.
- **B05 2026 update (3:20):** Pınarbaşı, Gough’s Cave, Kesslerloch; dogs already spread widely. PAYOFF C003/C004/C009.
- **B06 How it started? (3:10):** commensal vs adoption; refuse false certainty. COMPLICATE C010/C011.
- **B07 Why relationship could persist (2:20):** possible benefits, dog exchange, care. EXPLAIN C004/C008/C010.
- **B08 Social coevolution endpoint (1:40):** modern oxytocin gaze as consequence, not origin proof. EVIDENCE C012.
- **B09 Agriculture changes dog again (2:20):** starch adaptation as continued evolution. EVIDENCE C013/C014.
- **B10 Dogs and human movement (1:50):** parallel but not identical population histories. EXPLAIN C015/C016.
- **B11 What remains unknown (1:40):** location, number of domestications, missing wolf population. COMPLICATE C002/C006/C007.
- **B12 Ending (1:30):** no single conquest moment; lasting narrowing of interspecies distance. PAYOFF C001/C002/C009.

## Knowledge order
1. thuần hoá clarified early;
2. DNA cổ explained before genome argument;
3. genome defined inline;
4. hội sinh defined only when hypothesis appears;
5. oxytocin defined once in modern-context section.

## First-use plan
- genome: inline definition at first use.
- hội sinh: inline definition at first use, no repeated jargon.
- oxytocin: inline definition at first use.
- specialist population-genetics methods: omit from narration.

## Terminology/alias plan
Primary labels: “chó”, “sói/sói xám”. Avoid Latin names and method acronyms.

## Claim-occurrence map
Repeated claims must change story job:
- C002: hook mystery → final uncertainty payoff.
- C003: 2026 evidence only fully explained once.
- C008: first as burial evidence, later as care implication callback.
- C010: competing hypothesis section only; later references stay brief.

## Evidence escalation
morphology ambiguity → burial context → genome → ancient wolf comparison → 2026 cross-regional dog genomes → later dietary adaptation.

## Scope guardrails
No exact-origin verdict; no “first dog” claim for contested specimens; no behavioral origin claim presented as proven.

## Ending payoff
The strongest conclusion is not who approached first, but that the relationship was already socially and genetically established before agriculture and then kept reshaping both species’ shared environment.
