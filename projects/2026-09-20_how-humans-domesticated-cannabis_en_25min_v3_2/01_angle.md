# 01 — Angle Engine

Selected: **H1 contradiction + H2 transformation**, with the seed-selection mechanism as the spine.

Central question: **How did repeated human choices turn one multipurpose cannabis plant into radically different crop forms?**

Core transformation: multipurpose crop → repeated seed saving and movement → different selection pressures → fiber/seed-oriented and flower/chemical-oriented forms → complex modern diversity.

Opening contradiction: cannabis is now famous for intoxication, yet the clearest direct evidence for deliberate burning of potent cannabis comes thousands of years after the beginning of the domestication story suggested by genetics and early cultivation evidence.

Alternative angles considered:
- birthplace mystery;
- evidence ladder: seed → genome → residue;
- human-decision chain: every harvest is a vote;
- one species, different jobs.

Selection rationale: the chosen angle has the strongest general-audience hook while keeping evidence uncertainty explicit.

Ending payoff: the transformative technology was a person deciding which seeds got another season.

Out of scope: modern legalization politics, medical efficacy, modern cultivation instructions, potency optimization, and a full post-antiquity history.
