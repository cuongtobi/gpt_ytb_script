# 04 — Story + Knowledge Architecture

Central question: **How did repeated human choices turn one multipurpose cannabis plant into radically different crop forms?**

Core transformation: multipurpose plant → repeated seed saving → integrated crop → different selection pressures → divergent form and chemistry → movement/mixing → specialized crop forms.

Beat order:
1. **Contradiction hook:** modern drug stereotype versus much older domestication story.
2. **Mechanism:** domestication as a vote cast with seed.
3. **Genome evidence:** 110 accessions, DNA/genome grounding, ~12,000 BP model estimate with uncertainty.
4. **Archaeology:** why early cultivation leaves an incomplete record; Zhuzhai.
5. **Agricultural integration:** 2026 Shandong phytolith evidence.
6. **Multipurpose logic:** flexible household crop before extreme specialization.
7. **Different scoreboards:** fiber-oriented versus flower/chemical-oriented selection; ~4,000 BP divergence signal.
8. **Chemistry:** cannabinoids, THC and CBD introduced only after selection mechanism is clear.
9. **Haimenkou:** >800 seeds and overlapping modern ranges; the messy middle.
10. **Movement:** Eurasian spread as many local experiments, not one clean arrow.
11. **Psychoactive evidence ladder:** Yanghai then Jirzankal; CBN only when needed.
12. **Current uncertainty + payoff:** 2025 pangenome complicates a clean wild-ancestor story; end on the hand selecting seed.

Reveal map:
- ~12,000 BP: TEASE → EVIDENCE → CALLBACK.
- multipurpose-to-specialized: TEASE → EXPLAIN → TRANSFORM → CALLBACK.
- Jirzankal: TEASE → EVIDENCE/PAYOFF → CALLBACK.
- 2025 wild-relative uncertainty: COMPLICATE late, then one ending reminder.

Terminology policy: use **cannabis** by default; map **Cannabis sativa** once; avoid chemotype, landrace, cannabinoid synthase and basal cannabis in narration.

No production notes or storyboard artifacts are in scope.
