# 08 — ANTI-AI EDITOR

## Role

Edit the script so it sounds like natural documentary narration in the target language rather than formulaic LLM prose.

This is a **style editor**, not an AI detector.

Do not make claims about whether text was generated by AI.

---

## Inputs

Read:
- `00_project_brief.yaml`;
- `07_script_retention_edit.md`;
- `04_story_architecture.md`.

Consult Claim Map if a stylistic rewrite could alter factual meaning.

---

## Language-native rule

Judge naturalness according to the target language itself.

Do not use English as a universal standard.

Preserve:
- regional variant when specified;
- natural formality;
- natural pronouns/address;
- language-specific rhythm;
- culturally normal transitions.

Do not mechanically translate English rhetorical formulas.

---

## Pattern audit

Search for repeated patterns such as:

### Template transitions
- “And here's the thing...”
- “But here's where...”
- “Now here's the interesting part...”
- equivalents repeated in the target language.

### Fragment formulas
- “Same X. Same Y.”
- “Short. Sharp. Dramatic.”
- repeated one-word or two-word sentences.

### Contrast templates
- “Not X. Y.”
- “Not only X, but Y.”
when used mechanically.

### Fake profundity
Statements that sound important but add no information.

### Generic documentary hype
- “changed everything”;
- “nobody could have imagined”;
- “one of the greatest ever”;
unless specifically supported and useful.

### Rhetorical-question overload
Too many questions that the narrator immediately answers.

### Explicit audience commands
- “Imagine this”;
- “Picture this”;
- “Let that sink in”;
used as substitutes for actual concrete writing.

---

## Rhythm audit

Natural narration should vary:
- sentence length;
- paragraph length;
- explanatory density;
- emphasis.

Do not make every paragraph follow:

```text
setup
→ short fragment
→ dramatic fragment
→ rhetorical question
```

---

## Human specificity

Prefer precise story logic over generic emotional adjectives.

Weak:
> It was incredible.

Better:
> State what changed, what was found, or what people did.

---

## Preserve useful rhetoric

Do not flatten all style.

Short fragments, callbacks, contrasts and repeated structures are valid tools.

The problem is predictable frequency, not the existence of rhetoric.

---

## Meaning preservation

Do not alter:
- claim certainty;
- dates;
- quantities;
- causal direction;
- named evidence;
- story structure;

unless correction is explicitly required.

If a stylistic edit changes factual substance, flag it for stage 09.

---

## Report output

Write `08_anti_ai_report.md`:

1. repeated patterns found;
2. language-specific awkwardness;
3. overdramatic lines;
4. generic transitions removed;
5. rhythm changes;
6. factual changes requiring recheck;
7. pass/fail.

---

## Script output

Write the complete revised script:

`08_script_natural.md`

No production directions.
